# Default game controller bindings, tuned for the Steam Deck and other
# Xbox-layout gamepads. Copy this file to your config directory as
# "controller.txt" to customize it, or rebind controls in Preferences;
# a config-directory copy takes precedence over this one.
#
# Button numbers follow SDL_GameControllerButton:
#   0 A   1 B   2 X   3 Y   4 Back/View   5 Guide (reserved by Steam)
#   6 Start/Menu   7 L3 (left stick click)   8 R3 (right stick click)
#   9 LB   10 RB   11 D-pad up   12 D-pad down   13 D-pad left   14 D-pad right
#   16-19 Back paddles (Steam Deck L4/L5/R4/R5), when the OS exposes them
# Trigger axes follow SDL_GameControllerAxis, with 1 = positive direction:
#   2 right stick X   3 right stick Y   4 left trigger   5 right trigger
#
# Not listed here because they are built in:
#   Left stick steers the ship and thrusts when pushed past the trigger threshold.
#   LB (9) opens the radial menu for fleet orders, deploy/recall, hail, and cloak.
#   Right stick Y zooms the view in flight and scrolls lists in menus.
#   In menus the left stick moves the cursor between buttons and A activates.
